read_me.txt ● ● ●

i’m maryam, a product designer based in abuja, nigeria. i moved into design in 2021 and have spent the years since working on products across fintech, health, saas and e-commerce.

i’m especially interested in products with a lot going on behind the interface: different user roles, messy information, rules, handoffs and edge cases. i like figuring out how all of that should work, then making it straightforward for the person using it.

more recently i’ve been using ai tools to prototype and build working versions of my own ideas. it has made me more interested in the space between designing and building, because i can take an idea much further than a mockup.

when i’m not designing, i’m usually reading, cooking, baking, painting, watching nollywood or disappearing into some sci-fi rabbit hole. ✿
